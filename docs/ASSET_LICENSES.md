Their PNG derivatives are supplied for the sample PDF. They are illustrations, not photographs or evidence of real properties. You may use, modify and redistribute these original project assets with the delivered project. No third-party stock photographs, fonts from CDNs, logos, reviews or partnership claims are included.

## PDF fonts

`public/fonts/DejaVuSans.ttf` and `DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf` are DejaVu Fonts, from the installed DejaVu distribution. Full Bitstream Vera/DejaVu redistribution licence is in `public/fonts/LICENSE.txt`. They are embedded/subset by jsPDF in generated browser PDFs. The website itself uses system Arial/Helvetica and Georgia fonts.

## Dependencies

React, React DOM, React Router, Vite and jsPDF are open-source packages. Their dependency versions and registry integrity hashes are captured in `package-lock.json`; upstream package licence notices remain with npm-installed packages. No paid service is used. All app imagery and PDF font bytes are served locally.

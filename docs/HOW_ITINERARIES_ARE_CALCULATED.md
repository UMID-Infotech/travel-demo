# How the itinerary is calculated

The planner first validates the customer's entered destination, dates, stay days, travellers, child ages, rooms, hotel category, interests, activity choice, transport and meal plan. It converts the stay into hotel nights (four days means three nights), then calculates departure, destination arrival, base changes and home return using agency-configured journey times. It reserves transfers, check-in/out, meals, rest and safe return margins before choosing unique activities that fit opening times, nearby travel connections, the group's ages and selected interests. Unsuitable or unfitted activities remain visibly unscheduled and uncharged. Finally, it totals actual hotel nights × rooms × rates, assigned vehicles/transfers, scheduled activities and payable meals, deducting included items to avoid double charges. The same result supplies the review, final itinerary and PDF; no fixed customer price or runtime AI is used.

## Hinglish

Customer ki destination, date, days, travellers, child ages, hotel category, rooms, interests, activities, transport aur meals ki choices pehle validate hoti hain. Enter kiye gaye days se nights nikalti hain—4 days ka matlab 3 nights—aur admin ke travel times se departure, arrival aur return calculate hote hain. Transfers, hotel check-in/out, meals aur rest ka time reserve karne ke baad engine nearby travel time, opening hours, age restrictions aur interests ke according suitable activities fit karta hai; jo activity fit nahi hoti uska charge nahi lagta. Aakhir mein actual hotel nights, rooms, vehicle/transfer, scheduled activities aur payable meals ke editable rates jodkar estimated cost banti hai. Review, final itinerary aur PDF mein isi ek calculation ka result aata hai.
